Support

Drip Fantasy

Email hello@dripfantasy.com. A person reads it. Most things are answered the same day during the season.

What to include

  • Your league's name, and the week it happened in.
  • What you expected, and what happened instead.
  • The app version — it is at the bottom of the app's settings screen.
  • A screenshot, if a screen is showing something wrong.

The common ones

  • A score looks wrong. Say which player and which window; scoring is replayed from real play-by-play and a specific play is the fastest way to find it.
  • A waiver claim did not go through. The team screen lists every claim with the reason it settled the way it did.
  • A trade needs undoing. Your commissioner can reverse a completed trade from their console.
  • You want out of a league. League menu → leave. A commissioner has to hand the league over first.
  • Your data. Ask and we will send it or delete it — see the privacy page.

Rules

How the game itself works — windows, metrics, effects, power-ups — is in the rulebook.
