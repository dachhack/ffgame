# Outreach — creators and lieutenants

Founder (Oct 16): "I want to recruit fantasy football content creators and
professionals to join and create innovative leagues. I also want to recruit
lieutenants to be in charge of areas of the app … Lieutenants could be just
avid players or experts."

Two tracks, one sequence:

1. **Lieutenants** own one area of the app: its showcase league, its feedback,
   its how-to-play page. Recruit them first, starting with people already
   playing, because they know the app and creators will need them.
2. **Creators and pros** run a league on a format their audience doesn't have
   anywhere else, and get a lieutenant to help set it up.

Fill the `«placeholders»` before sending. The repo is public: keep target
names, handles and replies in the tracker (§6), not in this file.

---

## 1. The areas

Eight areas. The first six are the ones you named; 7 and 8 cover what's left.

| # | Area | What it covers | Good fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Drip** | The house format: windows, hidden metrics, effects (nuke, erase, hot streak), power-ups, the Window Pot | Someone who likes strategy games as much as fantasy |
| 2 | **Classic** | Redraft, keeper, dynasty, auction, FAAB, trades, IDP, player flags, contracts, the Taco Locker, commissioner tools | A longtime commissioner of a complicated home league |
| 3 | **Devy & College** | Devy spots, devy shares and the market, college lineups, adding D2/JUCO/HS players, the college card | A devy player who follows recruiting |
| 4 | **Relegation** | Linked-league ladders (promotion and relegation at season's end) and tiers inside one league (moves every 2–6 weeks) | A soccer fan, or someone running a big multi-league community |
| 5 | **DFS & Bullseye** | Salary-cap leagues, weekly squad contests, the open Bullseye contest (slots or total) | A DFS grinder who wants something new to solve |
| 6 | **Chaos formats** | Guillotine, Vampire, Golf (low score wins), Shotgun Wedding | Someone who already runs a guillotine or chopped league |
| 7 | **Other sports** | NBA, WNBA, NHL, MLB native leagues | A multi-sport player, especially basketball or baseball |
| 8 | **Commissioners & imports** | Bringing a league over from Sleeper, ESPN, Yahoo, MFL or Fleaflicker; onboarding new commissioners; the help docs | The person in every group chat who sets the league up |

One person can hold two areas while the program is small. Areas 1, 2 and 6 are
the most important to fill first: they have the most leagues.

**Can start this season:** DFS & Bullseye (weekly, any week), in-league
tiers (from the next period), Season Replay leagues. **Better pitched for
2027:** Devy, ladders, chaos formats, classic dynasty startups (spring and
summer drafts).

## 2. The lieutenant role (send this as the one-pager)

**Drip Fantasy lieutenant — «area»**

What you do, about an hour a week:

- **Run or play in the showcase league** for your area, so there is always one
  live example to point new people at.
- **Be the first reader of feedback** for your area. Each week, send the
  founder the three things that matter most: what broke, what confused people,
  what they asked for.
- **Try changes before they ship.** New features for your area come to you
  first.
- **Keep the how-to-play page honest.** If the rules page says one thing and
  the game does another, flag it.
- **Help creators set up** leagues in your area (§3).

What you get:

- A direct line to the founder and a real say in what gets built for your area.
- Personal premium free, in every league you're in. «confirm: and a league
  unlock for your showcase league»
- Your name in the release notes for what you shaped. «confirm: a lieutenant
  badge in the app — not built yet»

What it isn't: paid, a moderator job, or a commitment past the season. Step
down any time.

**Decide before sending:** the perks marked «confirm». Don't offer cash or
prizes tied to recruiting without checking `docs/commercialization-handoff.md`
§8 (real-money rules).

## 3. What creators get

- **A format their audience can't get on Sleeper or ESPN.** A relegation
  ladder across their Discord, a vampire league, a guillotine with Shotgun
  Wedding trades, a devy league with a share market, a Bullseye contest for the
  whole audience every week.
- **Setup done with them.** A lieutenant builds the league with them and
  watches the first week.
- **Premium for their league.** «confirm: league unlock free for the season»
- **A say in the format.** If they want a rule that doesn't exist, it can
  get built. Bullseye, Shotgun Wedding and the Taco Locker each went from one
  founder message to shipped in days. That turnaround is the pitch.
- **Content.** Week reports, chat cards and dramatic finishes (a nuke at
  11:58 on SNF) make segments.

Who to approach, by area:

| Area | Creator profile | Where they are |
|---|---|---|
| Devy & College | Devy rankers, CFB-to-NFL prospect analysts | Devy Discords, r/DynastyFF, YouTube devy shows |
| Relegation | Dynasty communities with several leagues already; soccer-crossover hosts | Large dynasty Discords, community league networks |
| DFS & Bullseye | DFS strategy writers and streamers | X/Bluesky DFS circles, DFS podcasts |
| Chaos formats | Hosts of guillotine, chopped and best-ball shows | YouTube, TikTok, podcast leagues |
| Classic / Drip | Podcasters who run a listener league every year | Podcast Discords, Patreon communities |
| Other sports | NBA and MLB fantasy creators in their off-months | Same platforms, sport-specific |
| Builders | Fantasy tool makers and data people | r/fantasyfootballcoding showcase threads |

Start with small and mid-sized creators (1k–50k followers). They answer DMs,
they need differentiated content, and one good league with them beats a
no-reply from a big name.

## 5. Sequence

| When | Do |
|---|---|
| Week 1 | Pick and DM lieutenant candidates from current players (4a), areas 1, 2, 6 first. Post 4b in-app. |
| Week 2 | Post 4b on Reddit/Discord for open areas. Send each new lieutenant the one-pager. |
| Weeks 2–3 | Each lieutenant names or confirms their area's showcase league. |
| Week 3 on | Creator DMs (4c), 5–10 a week, only for areas with a lieutenant. Lead with Bullseye/DFS in-season. |
| Dec–Feb | Lean on Devy, Relegation and chaos-format pitches for 2027 startups. |

One rule: **don't pitch a creator an area with no lieutenant.** Their league
is a showcase, and nobody will be there to catch its first-week problems.

## 6. Tracker

Keep it private (a sheet, not the repo). Columns:

`name · handle · track (lieutenant/creator) · area · audience size · channel ·
first contact · last contact · status (cold/replied/call/yes/no/later) · league
link · notes`

Signals to watch: reply rate per message, leagues started per creator "yes,"
and lieutenant weekly reports actually arriving. A lieutenant who misses three
weeks gets a friendly check-in, then the area reopens.
